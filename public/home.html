📖 How To Play

🎯 Game Objective

Grid Card Game is a tactical card battle where two players face off on a grid battlefield. Your goal is simple: destroy your opponent's heart before they destroy yours!

❤️
Protect Your Heart
Your heart has 30 HP. If it reaches 0, you lose the game!
⚔️
Attack the Enemy
Move your units forward and attack the enemy heart to win!
🃏
Play Cards
Summon units and cast spells using your energy each turn.
🧠
Think Tactically
Position matters! Use the grid wisely to outmaneuver your opponent.

Turn Structure

Each turn, you can perform these actions in any order:

🎴
Draw a Card
Once per turn, draw a card from your deck to add to your hand.
📤
Play Cards
Spend energy to play unit cards or spell cards from your hand.
👣
Move Units
Each unit can move one space (up, down, left, or right) per turn.
⚔️
Attack
Each unit can attack once per turn if an enemy is in range.
💡
Pro Tip: You don't have to use all your actions! Sometimes it's better to save energy or wait for a better position.

⚡ Random Tile Buffs

Each game, random tiles on the board are marked with special buffs! When you move a unit onto a buff tile, that unit receives a powerful bonus.

⚔️
Attack Buff
+2 ATK permanently added to the unit that lands on this tile.
💚
Health Buff
+3 HP permanently added to the unit that lands on this tile.
🔋
Energy Buff
Gain +1 energy at the start of each turn while you control a unit that got this buff.
👟
Move Buff
The unit can now move twice per turn instead of once!
💡
Strategy Tip: Race to grab buff tiles early! A well-buffed unit can turn the tide of battle. The buffs stay with the unit even after it leaves the tile.
⚠️
Important: Units can only attack the heart from the heart row (middle 3 columns of the enemy's back row).

🃏 Card Types

There are three types of cards you can play:

🗡️
Unit Cards
Soldiers, creatures, and fighters that go onto the battlefield. They have ATK and HP stats.
⚔️ ATK = Damage dealt ♥ HP = Health points
🏰
Structure Cards
Buildings and objects that provide ongoing effects. They have HP but usually can't attack. They stay on the board until destroyed.
✨
Spell Cards
Instant effects that happen immediately. They don't go on the board - just cast them for their effect!

Card Stats

💎
Energy Cost
The blue number in the corner. You need this much energy to play the card.
⚔️
Attack (ATK)
How much damage this unit deals when it attacks. Higher is better!
♥
Health Points (HP)
How much damage this unit can take before being destroyed.

Hand Limit

You can hold a maximum of 7 cards in your hand. If you already have 7 cards, you cannot draw - you must play a card first to make room!

⚡ Energy System

Energy is your resource for playing cards. Managing it wisely is key to victory!

⚔️ Combat System

Combat is straightforward but tactical. Understanding attack range is crucial!

Attack Range

🗡️
Melee Units (Range 1)
Most units attack adjacent enemies (1 square away in any direction including diagonals).
🏹
Ranged Units (Range 2+)
Archers and some aliens can attack from 2 or more squares away!

How Combat Works

1️⃣
Select Your Unit
Click on one of your units that hasn't attacked this turn.
2️⃣
Choose Target
Valid targets will be highlighted. Click an enemy unit or the heart row.
3️⃣
Deal Damage
Your unit's ATK is subtracted from the target's HP.
4️⃣
Check for Death
If HP reaches 0 or below, the unit is destroyed!
⚠️
No Counter-Attacks! Unlike some card games, defending units do NOT automatically hit back. Combat is one-directional.

Attacking the Heart

To damage the enemy heart, your unit must be in row 2 (one row in front of the enemy's back row) and in columns 2-5 (the middle 4 columns). Then attack toward the heart! The heart has 30 HP - reduce it to 0 to win!

💡
Rush Strategy: Fast, cheap units can sometimes slip past defenses and chip away at the heart. Don't always focus on killing every enemy unit!

✨ Card Effects & Abilities

Many cards have special effects that trigger at different times. Here are the common effect types:

Trigger Types

📤
Deploy Effect
Triggers when the card is first played onto the board.
💀
Death Effect
Triggers when this unit is destroyed.
🎯
On Kill Effect
Triggers when this unit kills an enemy.
🔄
Start of Turn
Triggers at the beginning of your turn.
⏹️
End of Turn
Triggers at the end of your turn.
♾️
Passive / Aura
Always active while this card is on the board.

Common Effects

💚
Heal
Restore HP to a unit or your heart.
💪
Buff ATK
Increase a unit's attack power.
🛡️
Damage Reduction
Reduce incoming damage by a set amount.
👶
Spawn
Create a new unit on the board.
🏹
Ranged
Attack from 2+ squares away.
👻
Untargetable
Cannot be targeted by attacks.
🦵
Double Move
Can move twice per turn.
⚡
Energy Drain
Steal or reduce enemy energy.

🏰 Campaign Mode

Battle through a series of increasingly challenging AI bosses to unlock new cards, decks, music, and backgrounds!

Boss Battles

Each boss has a unique deck theme and rewards. Defeat them to unlock their cards for your collection!

👽
The Void Scout
Defeat to unlock the Void Alien deck with energy-draining creatures!
🤠
The Dead Sheriff
Defeat to unlock the Western Skeleton deck with undead outlaws!
🧛
The Blood Countess
Defeat to unlock the Crimson Court vampire deck with lifesteal!

⚙️ Difficulty Settings

Choose your challenge level before each boss battle! Click the difficulty selector on a boss card to change it.

🟢
Easy
AI makes simpler decisions. Great for learning new decks or casual play.
🟡
Medium
Balanced challenge. AI plays strategically but isn't ruthless.
🔴
Hard
AI plays optimally, prioritizes threats, and punishes mistakes!

🎰 Victory Rewards & Lottery

When you defeat a boss, you'll spin the Card Lottery to win new cards!

⬜
Common Cards
Most frequent drops. You can own up to 3 copies of each common card.
🔷
Rare Cards
Less common, more powerful. You can own up to 2 copies of each rare card.
⭐
Legendary Cards
The rarest and strongest! You can only own 1 copy of each legendary.
💡
Lottery Tip: The lottery animation shows the card's rarity with special effects - watch for golden sparkles on legendary drops!

📚 Deck Building

Build custom decks from cards you've collected! Access the Deck Builder from the home screen.

Deck Rules

📊
Deck Size
Each deck must have 25-35 cards. Find the right balance!
🔢
Copy Limits
Max copies per card: 3 Common, 2 Rare, 1 Legendary
🎨
Card Sets
Cards belong to sets (Medieval, Void Alien, etc). You can mix cards from different sets!

Available Decks

⚔️
Medieval Kingdom
Classic knights, archers, and siege warfare. Always available to all players.
👽
Void Alien
Energy manipulation and swarm tactics. Unlock by defeating The Void Scout.
💀
Western Skeleton
Undead outlaws with death triggers. Unlock by defeating The Dead Sheriff.
🧛
Crimson Court
Vampires with lifesteal and soul theft. Unlock by defeating The Blood Countess.

🎵 Deck Customization

In the deck builder, you can customize more than just cards:

🎵
Battle Music
Choose which theme music plays during battles with this deck.
🖼️
Background
Select a battlefield background from themes you've unlocked.
✏️
Deck Name
Give your custom deck a unique name!

⚡ Energy Curve

The deck builder shows your energy curve - a chart showing how many cards you have at each cost. A good deck has a mix of cheap and expensive cards!

💡
Building Tip: Include plenty of 1-3 cost cards for early game, and a few powerful 4-6 cost cards for late game finishers!

💡 Strategy Tips

🎯
Control the Center
Units in the middle columns can respond to threats on either side. Don't bunch up on one side!
💰
Manage Your Energy
Don't overspend early! Save some energy for responses. A 0-energy turn means you can't play anything.
🃏
Draw Every Turn
Always draw a card if you can! Card advantage wins games. More options = better plays.
🏃
Advance with Purpose
Move forward to threaten the heart, but don't leave your own heart undefended!
🎭
Read Your Opponent
Watch what deck they're playing. Anticipate their big cards and plan counters.
🧮
Count Lethal
Always check if you can win this turn! Add up all your potential damage to the heart.
🛡️
Trade Efficiently
Use cheap units to kill expensive ones. A 2-cost unit killing a 5-cost unit is a great trade!
🎪
Use Structures Wisely
Structures provide ongoing value but can't attack. Place them where they won't block your units!

Beginner Mistakes to Avoid

❌
Forgetting to draw - You miss out on cards and fall behind!
❌
Playing everything immediately - Save some cards and energy for defense!
❌
Ignoring the heart - You can lose while having board control if you don't defend!
🏆
Most importantly: Have fun and experiment! Try different strategies and learn from each game. Good luck, commander!
